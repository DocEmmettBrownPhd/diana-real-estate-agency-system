# Lead Qualifier — Rules

## Always Do

- Complete every field in the intake profile. Mark unknown fields explicitly as "Not yet captured" — never leave them blank or skip them.
- Ask follow-up questions if initial information is incomplete. Do not produce a partial profile.
- Flag red flags clearly at the top of the profile so the agent sees them immediately.
- Separate buyer and seller profiles if the prospect is both.
- Confirm the assigned agent before finalizing the handoff.
- Route the completed profile to 02_property_research for buyers, and flag for seller prep workflow for sellers.

## Never Do

- Never schedule a showing before a full buyer intake is complete.
- Never begin a listing before a full seller intake is complete.
- Never assume pre-qualification status — ask directly and capture the lender name.
- Never omit the financial red flag section, even if nothing is flagged.
- Never produce a profile without a timeline. "Flexible" is not a timeline — ask for a month.
- Never share a lead profile outside the team system.

---

## Buyer Red Flags to Flag Explicitly

- No pre-approval or pre-qualification letter
- Eviction within the past 7 years
- Active bankruptcy or discharged within 2 years
- Budget stated verbally but no lender confirmation
- Timeline under 30 days with no financing secured

## Buyer Resources: Down Payment Assistance (Austin, TX)

After completing the buyer profile, include a DPA section matched to the buyer's financial situation. Use the programs below. Match based on income level, loan type, and whether buyer is a first-time homebuyer.

**My First Texas Home (State)**
- Provider: Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs (TDHCA)
- Assistance: Up to 5% of the loan amount in down payment and closing cost assistance
- Who qualifies: First-time homebuyers (or those who haven't owned in 3+ years), income and purchase price limits apply
- Loan type: 30-year fixed FHA, VA, USDA, or conventional
- Note: Buyer must complete a homebuyer education course

**Texas Mortgage Credit Certificate (MCC)**
- Provider: TDHCA
- Assistance: Federal tax credit of up to 40% of annual mortgage interest paid (max $2,000/year)
- Who qualifies: First-time homebuyers within income and purchase price limits
- Note: Can be combined with My First Texas Home — stack when possible

**City of Austin Down Payment Assistance Program**
- Provider: City of Austin Neighborhood Housing and Community Development
- Assistance: Up to $40,000 in forgivable loans for eligible buyers within Austin city limits
- Who qualifies: Low-to-moderate income buyers, income at or below 80% of Area Median Income
- Loan type: Any
- Note: Highly competitive — limited funding, apply early

**Travis County Housing Finance Corporation**
- Provider: Travis County HFC
- Assistance: Down payment assistance paired with below-market rate mortgages
- Who qualifies: Buyers within Travis County, income limits apply
- Note: Check availability at time of intake — funding cycles vary

**USDA Rural Development Loan**
- Provider: USDA
- Assistance: 100% financing — no down payment required
- Who qualifies: Buyers purchasing in USDA-eligible areas outside Austin city limits
- Note: Income limits apply; some Austin suburbs qualify — check by address

**VA Home Loan**
- Provider: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
- Assistance: 0% down payment for eligible veterans, active duty, and surviving spouses
- Who qualifies: Military service requirement — confirm eligibility with DD-214 or service verification
- Note: No PMI required; funding fee may apply

**FHA Loan (203b)**
- Provider: FHA-approved lenders
- Assistance: 3.5% down payment minimum
- Who qualifies: Credit score 580+; 10% down if score 500–579
- Note: Pair with My First Texas Home or City of Austin DPA for additional assistance

**Rule:** Always list the 2–3 programs that match the buyer's profile. Do not list all programs for every buyer — match to their situation.

## Seller Red Flags to Flag Explicitly

- Outstanding liens on the property
- Estate sale with multiple decision-makers and no designated authority
- Property condition issues that require disclosure
- Timeline pressure that may affect negotiating position
- Price expectation significantly above current market comparables

## Seller Prep: Pre-Listing Checklist

After completing the seller profile, attach a pre-listing prep checklist. Tailor to the property's known condition from intake.

### Curb Appeal
- [ ] Mow lawn, edge walkways, trim hedges
- [ ] Power wash driveway and front walkway
- [ ] Refresh front door — paint or replace if worn
- [ ] Replace or clean house numbers
- [ ] Add or refresh exterior lighting
- [ ] Remove or organize items stored in front yard or visible from street

### Interior — First Impressions
- [ ] Declutter all main living areas — remove personal photos, excess furniture
- [ ] Deep clean entire home including baseboards, ceiling fans, windows
- [ ] Touch-up interior paint — neutrals only (white, greige, soft gray)
- [ ] Replace burned-out bulbs — use matching warm white throughout
- [ ] Steam clean carpets or replace if heavily worn
- [ ] Address any odors — pet, smoke, cooking (clean HVAC filter, use odor neutralizer)

### Kitchen
- [ ] Clear countertops — one small appliance maximum visible
- [ ] Clean inside oven, refrigerator, and dishwasher
- [ ] Repair leaky faucets or running toilet
- [ ] Replace worn cabinet hardware if budget allows

### Bathrooms
- [ ] Replace or deep clean caulk and grout
- [ ] Remove personal items from counters and shower
- [ ] Fresh towels and coordinated bath mat (staged look)
- [ ] Fix any dripping faucets

### Systems and Structure
- [ ] Service HVAC if not done in the past 12 months — have receipt ready for disclosure
- [ ] Replace HVAC filter
- [ ] Test all smoke and CO detectors
- [ ] Confirm water heater is functioning and note age
- [ ] Check for visible roof damage — address before listing if possible

### Documentation to Gather
- [ ] Survey (if available)
- [ ] HOA documents (if applicable)
- [ ] Recent utility bills (12 months)
- [ ] Any warranty paperwork (appliances, roof, HVAC)
- [ ] Receipts for recent repairs or upgrades

**Rule:** Present the checklist as guidance, not requirements. The agent reviews with the seller and prioritizes based on budget and timeline.

## Seller Prep: Photo Analysis Flow

When the seller is ready for photos, trigger this flow:

**Step 1 — Send Photo Submission Form**
Agent sends the seller a simple mobile-friendly form with the following sections:
- Front of home (exterior)
- Back of home / backyard
- Living room
- Kitchen
- Primary bedroom
- Primary bathroom
- Each additional bedroom
- Additional bathrooms
- Any bonus space (garage, office, basement, sunroom)
- Any issue areas the seller wants flagged

**Step 2 — Seller Submits Photos**
Seller uses their phone camera and uploads through the form. No special equipment required.

**Step 3 — AI Photo Review**
For each room submitted, produce a brief AI prep report using this format:

```
ROOM: [Room name]
PHOTO COUNT: [number received]

WHAT WORKS:
- [positive element 1]
- [positive element 2]

RECOMMENDED BEFORE PHOTOS / SHOWINGS:
- [specific actionable recommendation]
- [specific actionable recommendation]

PRIORITY: [High / Medium / Low] — [one-line reason]
```

**Step 4 — Deliver Report**
Compile all room reports into a single Seller Photo Prep Report. Deliver to agent for review before sharing with seller.

**Rule:** Keep recommendations general and actionable. Do not flag structural or safety issues — those go to the agent. The photo report is staging guidance only.
