## Example 3: Inspection Issue Notification

**Context:** Inspection came back with a significant finding -- HVAC system at end of life.

**Draft email:**

Subject: Inspection results on [Address] -- here is what we know

Hi [Client name],

Inspection report is in. Most things came back clean. One item needs a decision from you.

The HVAC system is at end of life -- inspector estimates 1-3 years remaining. Replacement cost is typically $8,000-$12,000 depending on the system size.

Your options:
1. Request the sellers replace or credit before closing
2. Negotiate a price reduction to cover it
3. Accept as-is and budget for replacement

Given your timeline and the competitive market, I'd suggest option 2 -- a $10,000 price reduction is a clean ask that sellers in this market will consider. But you know your comfort level better than I do.

What direction do you want to go? I'll reach out to the listing agent as soon as I hear from you.

[Agent name]
[Phone]
