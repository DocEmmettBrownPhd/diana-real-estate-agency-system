# CliffNotes Weekly Competition #4 — Submission

I built an eight-specialist AI operating system for a boutique Austin real estate team. Every specialist owns one job — lead qualification, property research, client communication, transaction coordination, compliance, and vendor intelligence — with explicit structured handoffs between them. Nothing bleeds. A new agent is operational in a day.

The key design decision: the Vendor Team predicts who you need before you think to ask. It reads the deal stage and property age and surfaces the right inspector, contractor, or stager — with name and phone — automatically.
