# CliffNotes Weekly Competition #4 — Submission

I built a six-specialist AI operating system for a boutique Austin real estate team. Every specialist owns one job and nothing bleeds between them.

The design decision I'm most confident in is the buyer and seller prep funnel. Before the agent ever gets involved, the buyer is matched to down payment assistance programs and lenders. The seller gets a prep checklist and a photo-based staging report. The agent walks in ready to work — not chasing paperwork, not explaining basics.

One more week and I'd add the ability for buyers to take pictures of homes they visit and get an instant report back.
