The Nine Specialists

Each One Owns a Defined Piece

No specialist does another's job. Every handoff is a labeled field block — not a paragraph summary — so any agent can pick up a deal mid-stream.

000

Agent Onboarding

Visual system overview and day-one orientation. Every new team member reads this folder before touching a live deal. Used once — then the agent is operational.

Day 1 only
00a · NEW

Lead Intake

The front door. Catches leads from any source in any format — Zillow email, phone call notes, old contact list, open house sign-in. Standardizes into one consistent Lead Package. Assigns to the right agent using fair rotation and workload balancing. Diana gets an Assignment Log for full visibility.

Front of funnel
00

Orchestrator

Reads every incoming request and routes it to the right specialist. Never does the work itself. Holds the routing table for all nine specialists and enforces the rule that no lead skips intake and no signed document skips compliance.

Router
01

Lead Qualifier

Deep buyer and seller intake. Every financial field, every timeline detail, every red flag documented before a showing is scheduled or a listing is taken. Matches buyers to Austin/Texas down payment assistance programs. Recommends lenders matched to loan type. Produces seller pre-listing checklist and photo staging report.

Buyer · Seller
02

Property Research

Comparables, neighborhood data, school ratings, and market analysis — all built around the specific client profile from the qualifier. Never produces generic research. Every brief is matched to what this buyer or seller actually needs.

Research
03

Client Communication

Emails, texts, and follow-up sequences written in the voice of the agent on the deal — not generic AI language. Handles reactivation outreach for cold leads, first-contact messages, option period updates, and closing day communications.

Outreach
04

Transaction Coordinator

Once a deal is live, this specialist owns the timeline. Tracks all deadlines, documents, and deliverables. Flags risks before they become problems. Produces a clear checklist of who owes what and by when — updated at every milestone.

Live deal
05

Compliance

Post-signature document review. Every executed document routes here before filing. Reviews for missing signatures, incorrect dates, and Texas-specific disclosure requirements. Knows TREC forms by name: IABS 1-0, OP-H, 36-10, HUD/EPA lead paint, MUD Notice. Produces a sign-off checklist for each transaction.

Post-signature · Texas
06

Vendor Team

Predicts which vendors are needed before the agent has to think about it. Reads the deal stage, property age, and inspection findings — then surfaces the right contacts (name and phone) from Diana's curated vendor list. Covers inspectors, surveyors, HVAC, plumbers, electricians, roofers, foundation, handyman, stager, photographer, title, and attorney.

Request Flow

How a Lead Moves Through the System

Three scenarios — a new buyer from Zillow, a seller call with gaps, and an old contact reactivation.

Scenario A — New Buyer · Zillow Email Forward

1
00a Lead Intake

Agent pastes the raw Zillow email

Lead Intake extracts name, phone, email, property of interest, and message. Flags any missing fields. Checks active lead counts — assigns to the next agent in fair rotation. Assignment logged for Diana.

2
01 Lead Qualifier

Deep intake on the assigned lead

Full buyer profile: pre-approval status, budget, target neighborhoods, must-haves, current living situation, eviction history, timeline. DPA programs and lenders matched to their profile.

3
02 Property Research

Research brief built for this buyer

Comparables in their target area, neighborhood walkability, school ratings, recent sales data — matched to their specific profile and must-haves.

4
03 Client Communication

First-contact message drafted

Email or text written in the assigned agent's voice. References specific properties from the research brief. Includes a clear next step.

5
04 Transaction Coordinator

Deal goes live — timeline starts

Deadlines, document checklist, and risk flags tracked from contract to close. Every milestone logged.

6
06 Vendor Team

Option Period — vendors surfaced automatically

Property age and condition flags trigger vendor recommendations. Inspector, surveyor, and any specialists flagged by the property — name and phone, ready to call.

7
05 Compliance

Every executed document reviewed before filing

Post-signature audit. Texas TREC forms verified. Missing signatures and disclosure gaps flagged. Sign-off checklist produced.

Scenario B — Phone Call with Missing Info

1
00a Lead Intake

Agent types their call notes — gaps flagged immediately

Intake extracts what was captured. Critical missing fields (no phone number, no last name) flagged as blockers. Lead assigned but held — not routed to qualifier until gaps are filled.

2
Hold at intake

Lead waits — agent knows exactly what to collect

When the lead calls back or the agent reaches them, the gap fields are filled and the Lead Package is completed. Only then does it move to the qualifier.

3
01+ onwards

Normal flow resumes

Qualifier runs the full intake. Deal moves forward through the standard specialist sequence.

Scenario C — Old Contact List Reactivation

1
00a Lead Intake

Agent pastes multiple rows — batch assigned

Each contact gets a Lead Package. Assignment Log shows all three at once. Referral leads go to Diana. Rotation leads spread across the team.

2
03 Client Communication

Reactivation outreach drafted first

Cold leads don't go to the qualifier until they re-engage. Communication specialist drafts a warm check-in for each one.

3
01 Lead Qualifier

Full intake once the lead re-engages

Normal flow from there. The qualifier picks up where the old contact data left off.

Design Decisions

Why It's Built This Way

Every major choice in this system has a reason. Here are the eight decisions that define the architecture.

Lead intake as the true front door

Leads arrive in every format — a forwarded Zillow email, phone call notes, a row from an old spreadsheet. The intake specialist standardizes all of it before anything else runs. No lead enters the system unformatted.

Fair distribution built into the architecture

Four agents, one team. Intake tracks who has what, rotates fairly, and surfaces capacity problems to Diana before they become missed opportunities. Diana gets an Assignment Log she can override at any time.

Agent onboarding before any live deal

The 000 folder exists so no agent touches a client without understanding the system. It is the first stop — not an afterthought. One read-through and the agent is operational.

Buyer and seller prep built into the funnel

Before the agent shows a property or takes a listing, the buyer is matched to DPA programs and lenders. The seller gets a prep checklist and a photo-based staging report. The agent walks in ready — not chasing paperwork.

Vendor Team as intelligence, not a directory

The Vendor Team doesn't wait to be asked. It reads the deal stage, property age, and inspection findings — then tells the agent exactly who to call, with name and number, before the agent has to think about it.

Structured handoffs over free text

Every specialist passes a labeled field block to the next one — not a paragraph summary. Any team member can pick up a deal mid-stream without calling Diana. The format is the documentation.

Compliance after signature only

Document review happens when executed documents return — not before. This is where errors and missing signatures actually matter. Catching them before filing protects the team from liability.

Texas-specific throughout

The compliance specialist knows Texas's required forms by name — TREC forms, Option Period timing, effective date math, MUD Notice, HUD/EPA lead paint. No generic national checklists. Austin team, Austin rules.

Getting Started

How to Run This in Claude

No software to install. No platform to learn. The folders are the system.

Claude Projects Setup — 5 Steps

1

Open Claude (claude.ai) and sign in.

2

Click Projects in the left sidebar.

3

Create a new Project — name it "Diana's Real Estate System".

4

Upload all specialist folders as Project Knowledge. Every conversation in this project has all nine specialists loaded — you never re-upload.

5

Start your first conversation by telling Claude which specialist you need.

You are the Lead Intake specialist. A new lead just came in from Zillow. Here is the email: [paste the forwarded email] --- You are the Lead Qualifier. The intake is complete. Here is the Lead Package: [paste the Lead Package from intake] --- You are the Orchestrator. New request: [describe what just happened in the deal]
