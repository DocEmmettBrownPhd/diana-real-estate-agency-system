How to Use This System

Four steps. Every deal, every time. The system does the heavy lifting — you make the calls.

1

Open Claude

Go to claude.ai on any device. No special setup required.

2

Start with the Orchestrator

Always start here. It routes you to the right specialist for your situation.

You are the Orchestrator for a real estate AI system. Read 00_orchestrator/identity.md. New client: [name + situation]
3

Follow the Handoff

Each specialist gives you a structured handoff. Paste it into the next specialist to continue the deal.

4

You Approve. You Send.

The AI drafts. You review. You make the final call before anything goes to the client or title.

⚡ Golden Rule

Never send an AI draft to a client without reading it first. You are the agent. You are responsible.

🔒 Compliance Rule

Only run the Compliance specialist after a document comes back signed. Never before.

🔄 Fresh Start Rule

Start a new Claude session for each new client. Never paste Client A's profile into Client B's thread.

📞 Escalation Rule

If a client is upset, the deal is falling apart, or a legal question comes up — call Diana. Do not use AI.

Clarity

Who Does What

The AI does the research, drafting, and tracking. You do the judgment, relationship, and final decisions. This line does not blur.

AI Handles

  • 📋 Building buyer and seller profiles
  • 🏘️ Neighborhood and comp research
  • ✉️ Drafting all client messages
  • 📅 Tracking contract milestone dates
  • 📝 Reviewing compliance on executed docs
  • 📸 Reviewing home photos against prep checklist
  • 🏦 Matching buyers to DPA programs
  • 🔄 Routing between specialists

You Handle

  • 📞 First contact and relationship building
  • 🏠 Showing homes and walking properties
  • 💼 Writing and negotiating offers
  • ✅ Reviewing and approving every AI draft
  • ⚖️ Legal and ethics questions
  • 😥 Emotional or distressed client conversations
  • 🚩 Deciding what to do when compliance flags a doc
  • 📬 Actually sending every message to the client

When Something Goes Wrong

Most situations have a clear next step. Here are the ones new agents hit most often.

Stop — Human Required

Compliance Returns FLAGGED

The compliance specialist found a blocking issue — missing signature, wrong date, missing disclosure. Do not send to title.

Call Diana immediately
Escalate

Client Is Upset or Emotional

AI does not handle emotional situations. If a buyer is panicking or a seller is angry — pick up the phone.

You handle this one
Escalate

Legal Question from Client

Client asks about contract terms, fair housing, or legal rights. Never answer with AI-drafted text. Refer to an attorney.

Refer to attorney

Do not send — verify first
