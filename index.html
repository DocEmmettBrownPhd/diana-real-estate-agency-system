What is Claude?

If you have used ChatGPT before, Claude is the same idea — but trained differently. Think of it as a very smart assistant that reads and responds to whatever you type.

Claude is like a new team member

You type instructions to it. It reads them and produces work — profiles, research, emails, compliance checklists. It does not make phone calls, go to showings, or sign anything. You do all of that.

You access it through your browser

Go to claude.ai on your phone or computer. Sign up with your email. It is free to start. No app to install. Just a website — like Gmail or Google.

You type. It responds.

Every conversation starts with you typing a message. The AI reads it and types back. You keep going back and forth just like texting — except the other person is an AI specialist that knows real estate.

This system has 6 specialists built in

The folders in this repo are the AI's instructions. When you tell Claude "you are the Lead Qualifier," it reads those instructions and plays that role. You switch roles by telling it which specialist it is.

🌐 Where to go

claude.ai — works on any phone or computer browser. Bookmark it now.

📧 How to sign up

Click "Sign up" and use your work email. Takes 2 minutes. Free account is enough to start.

💬 How it works

Type your message. Hit enter or click send. Claude types back. That is the whole interface.

🔄 New client = new chat

Start a fresh conversation for every new client. Click "New conversation" in the sidebar.

⚡ One Rule Above All

Never send anything the AI writes directly to a client. You read it, you edit it, you send it. You are the agent. The AI is your assistant.

🔒 Compliance is Post-Signature Only

Only run the Compliance specialist after a client signs a document and sends it back. Never before.

🔄 One Client Per Chat

Always start a new conversation for each new client. Never mix two clients in the same thread.

Your AI Team

The 6 Specialists

Each specialist does one job and one job only. You do not need to explain the system to it — just tell it who it is and give it the client information.

🎯
SPECIALIST 00

Orchestrator

The traffic controller. Reads any situation and tells you which specialist to use. Start here when you are not sure where to go. It asks one question if needed, then routes you.

▶ Use when: You are not sure which specialist to activate
👤
SPECIALIST 01

Lead Qualifier

Builds the full profile for every new lead. For buyers: budget, must-haves, DPA program match, lender recommendation. For sellers: prep checklist, photo analysis. All documented before you make a single call.

▶ Use when: A new buyer or seller lead comes in
🏘️
SPECIALIST 02

Property Research

Runs the neighborhood analysis and pulls comparable sales based on the buyer's exact criteria. Delivers school ratings, market trends, days on market, and a scored match report — so you walk into the search ready.

▶ Use when: Buyer is approved and ready to start searching
✉️
SPECIALIST 03

Client Communication

Drafts every message to your client — showing confirmations, offer updates, inspection results, financing delays, next-step emails. Produces both an email version and a text version. You review, edit, and send. It never sends for you.

▶ Use when: You need to send a message to your client
📅
SPECIALIST 04

Transaction Coordinator

Takes over the moment a contract is signed. Tracks every Texas deadline — Effective Date, Option Period, earnest money at title, appraisal, clear-to-close, final walkthrough. Flags risks before they become problems. The deal ends here: closing table or termination.

▶ Use when: A contract is executed (both sides signed)
✅
SPECIALIST 05

Compliance Review

Post-signature document review only. When a signed contract or disclosure comes back, this specialist checks every field — TREC form numbers, signatures, initials, dates, required Texas disclosures. Returns CLEARED (safe to file) or FLAGGED (do not send to title).

▶ Use when: A signed document comes back from the client
Common Mistakes

Do This. Not This.

These are the mistakes new agents make most often. Read them once and you will not make them.

❌ Not This

❌

Copy the AI draft and send it directly to the client

The AI does not know your client personally. You do. Always read and edit before sending.
❌

Run Compliance before the document comes back signed

Compliance only runs post-signature. Running it early produces useless output.
❌

Paste Client A's profile into Client B's conversation

Always start a fresh new conversation for every new client. Mixed context = wrong output.
❌

Skip the Orchestrator when you are not sure what to do

That is exactly what it is for. Going to the wrong specialist wastes time.
❌

Use AI to answer a legal question from a client

Refer legal questions to an attorney. Always. No exceptions.
❌

Use AI when a client is upset or distressed

Emotional situations need a human voice. Pick up the phone.

✅ Do This

✅

Read every AI draft before it goes anywhere

You are the agent. You are responsible. The AI assists. You decide.
✅

Only send signed documents to Compliance

Wait until the executed document comes back. Then run Compliance immediately.
✅

Start a new conversation for every new client

Click "New conversation" each time. One client per chat thread, always.
✅

Go to the Orchestrator first when unsure

Type one sentence about the situation. It routes you to the right specialist immediately.
✅

Refer legal questions to an attorney

"That is a great question — let me connect you with our attorney." Done.
✅

Call Diana when something does not feel right

No question is too small. If it feels off, it probably is. Escalate.

Who Does What

The AI handles the research, drafting, and tracking. You handle the judgment, the relationship, and every final decision. This line never blurs.

🤖 The AI Handles This
  • 📋 Building buyer and seller profiles
  • 🏘️ Neighborhood research and comparable sales
  • ✉️ Drafting all client emails and texts
  • 📅 Tracking every contract deadline
  • 📝 Compliance review on executed documents
  • 📸 Reviewing home photos against prep checklist
  • 🏦 Matching buyers to DPA programs and lenders
  • 🔄 Routing between specialists automatically
🧑 You Handle This
  • 📞 First contact and relationship building
  • 🏠 Showing homes and walking properties
  • 💼 Writing and negotiating offers
  • ✅ Reviewing and approving every AI draft
  • ⚖️ Legal and ethics questions

  • 📬 Actually pressing send on every message
Troubleshooting

When Something Goes Wrong

Every situation below has a clear next step. When in doubt — call Diana.

🛑 Stop Immediately

Compliance Returns FLAGGED

A blocking issue was found — missing signature, wrong date, or missing disclosure. Do not send to title. Do not proceed.

Call Diana now
📞 Escalate

Client Is Upset or Emotional

AI does not handle emotional conversations. If a buyer is panicking or a seller is upset — put down the keyboard and pick up the phone.

You handle this one
📞 Escalate

Client Asks a Legal Question

Fair housing, contract rights, disclosure obligations. Never answer with AI text. Always refer to an attorney.

Refer to attorney
⚠️ Pause and Check

AI Response Looks Off

Dates that do not add up, names that are wrong, details that do not match what the client told you. Trust your gut.

Do not send — verify first
⚠️ Use the Orchestrator

Not Sure Which Specialist to Use

That is exactly what the Orchestrator is for. Describe the situation in one sentence and it will route you.

Start with Orchestrator (00)
📞 Escalate

Negotiation Over Ask Price

Multiple offers, escalation clauses, or complex counter terms require Diana's judgment. The AI does not negotiate.

Call Diana

📞 Diana — Call Anytime

Complex negotiations, flagged compliance, anything that does not feel right. No question is too small. That is what she is there for.

⚖️ Attorney — Legal Questions Only

Any client question about contract rights, fair housing, or disclosure requirements. Refer out. Never use AI for legal answers.

🏦 Lender — Financing Questions

The AI identifies which DPA program and lender fits the buyer. The lender answers the actual financing questions.
