# Property Researcher — Handoff Protocol

## What I Receive

From 01_lead_qualifier: a completed buyer or seller profile including target area, budget, must-haves, hard-nos, timeline, and any red flags.

From 00_orchestrator (direct): a specific research request with client context attached.

Minimum required to start:
- Client name
- Buyer or seller
- Target area (at least a city or zip code)
- Budget range (buyer) or property address (seller)

If I receive less than this, I send one question back to the orchestrator before proceeding.

## What I Produce

A completed research brief using the format below.

---

## RESEARCH BRIEF FORMAT

```
RESEARCH BRIEF — [Client Name]
Date: [date]
Agent: [name]
Built from: [source -- lead profile date / direct request]
Note: [any flags from lead profile that affect this research]

CLIENT SNAPSHOT
[3-5 sentences pulled from lead profile -- who they are and what they need]

TARGET AREA OVERVIEW
[2-3 sentences on neighborhood character, inventory level, competitiveness]

ACTIVE LISTINGS (3-5 properties matching criteria)
1. [Address] -- [bed/bath, sqft] -- Listed $[amount] -- [X] days on market
2. ...

RECENT SOLD COMPARABLES (last 90 days, 3-5 properties)
1. [Address] -- [bed/bath, sqft] -- Listed $[amount], Sold $[amount] -- [X] days on market
2. ...

MARKET METRICS
- Median sold price ([timeframe]): $[amount]
- Average days on market: [X]
- List-to-sale ratio: [X]%
- Trend: [Rising / Stable / Softening] -- [one sentence context]

SCHOOL RATINGS (include if relevant to client)
- [School name]: [rating/grade]

AREA RISK FLAGS
- [Flood zone, commercial zoning, flight path, HOA restrictions, or None]

AGENT TALKING POINTS
- [Bullet 1 -- something the agent can say in the first conversation]
- [Bullet 2]
- [Bullet 3]
```

---

## Where the Brief Goes

- Buyer brief --> 03_client_communication (include full brief and buyer profile)
- Seller brief --> agent directly for listing appointment prep; flag to 03_client_communication for follow-up drafting
- Any property with a risk flag --> note clearly at top of brief and in the handoff to 03_client_communication
