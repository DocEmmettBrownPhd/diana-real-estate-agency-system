## THE HANDOFF CHAIN

Every specialist passes a structured block to the next one. No loose summaries. No assumptions.

```
01 LEAD QUALIFIER
        |
        |  passes: BUYER PROFILE or SELLER PROFILE
        |  (includes: DPA match, lender list, prep checklist)
        v
02 PROPERTY RESEARCH
        |
        |  passes: RESEARCH BRIEF
        |  (includes: comps, market metrics, risk flags, talking points)
        v
03 CLIENT COMMUNICATION
        |
        |  passes: COMMUNICATION DRAFT
        |  (includes: email draft, text draft, situation context)
        v
04 TRANSACTION COORDINATOR
        |
        |  passes: DEAL STATUS REPORT or DEADLINE ALERT
        |  (includes: full timeline, document checklist, risk flags)
        v
05 COMPLIANCE
        |
        |  passes: SIGN-OFF CHECKLIST or FLAGGED REVIEW
        |  (includes: Georgia disclosure verification, Binding Agreement Date check)
        v
     FILED / RESOLVED
```

## THE ONE RULE

**Nothing bleeds between specialists.**

The lead qualifier does not research properties.
The researcher does not draft emails.
The communicator does not track deadlines.
The transaction coordinator does not review compliance.
The compliance specialist does not draft documents.

Every specialist owns their lane. Stay in it.
That is what makes the system work.
